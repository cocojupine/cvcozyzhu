# Personal Website Redesign Brief

## Objective

Redesign the homepage as a personal website that prioritizes future job opportunities, with collaboration opportunities as a secondary benefit. The site should create a memorable, human identity without discarding the mature career information, reviewed copy, metrics, and project evidence already present.

This is not a rewrite for its own sake. Existing content that already works should be retained.

## Fixed Hero Meaning

### Chinese

**一个带着黑客松精神做产品的人。**

兴趣把我带向新的问题，行动让我把想法做出来，而商业化思维让我判断，什么值得成为真正的产品。

### English intent

The English version may be polished independently, but it must retain the same meaning: hackathon-minded curiosity and action, balanced by product and commercial judgment.

## Personal Identity

- Curious about innovation and learning new knowledge.
- Energized by making ideas tangible.
- Brings action, creative energy, and momentum to a team.
- Uses product and commercial judgment to decide what deserves to become a real product.
- Open to cross-cultural exchange and international collaboration, expressed naturally through existing experience rather than a dedicated global-identity section.

## Content Rules

- Preserve the current homepage's mature, reviewed descriptions.
- Preserve bilingual content, quantified outcomes, career history, project media, dark technical character, and the contact dock.
- Preserve the current experience format: company, role, period, tags, and detailed contribution.
- Preserve the current project-card descriptions and evidence.
- Do not replace specific, credible business and contribution details with vague personality claims.
- Do not over-focus on business mechanics when describing experience. Let the page reveal the surrounding system, personal contribution, outcome, and demonstrated capability together.
- Keep all four competency areas and their existing claims. Their visual organization may change, but their definitions should not be rewritten merely to appear new.
- Projects remain evidence of capability, but should not dominate the homepage narrative or expand into long case studies on the homepage.
- Do not add a job-seeking manifesto, collaboration invitation, or `THE NEXT THING, TOGETHER` section.
- Resume, social links, and the existing contact dock remain the paths for follow-up.

## First-Round Scope

- Homepage only.
- High-fidelity, interactive concepts; full production hardening is deferred until a direction is selected.
- All three concepts use the same fixed Hero meaning, facts, and source content.
- All three concepts may reorder sections to test narrative rhythm.
- Visual assets may differ. Apple Avatar is one option, not a requirement.
- Project detail pages will be unified and completed after a homepage direction is selected.

## Three Experiments

1. **Skill-led** — faithfully use the installed `frontend-design` Skill.
2. **Independent editorial direction** — design freely from the shared brief, with strong personal authorship.
3. **Bold experimental direction** — explore a more surprising narrative rhythm, interaction model, and visual asset strategy without changing the underlying identity or facts.

## Comparison Criteria

- Is the person memorable after a short visit?
- Does the site preserve professional credibility and recruiter scanability?
- Do curiosity, action, learning, and commercial judgment feel real rather than asserted?
- Are existing strengths easier to notice?
- Does the narrative feel like a personal website rather than a generic resume or project showcase?
- Does the visual system remain coherent when presenting dense career information?
